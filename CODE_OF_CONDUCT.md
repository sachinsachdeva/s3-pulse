# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, critique the work rather than
the person, and accept that reasonable people disagree about engineering
trade-offs.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing others'
private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space,
and to public spaces when representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainers through
GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or
reject contributions that conflict with this document, and may block accounts
for sustained or severe breaches.

This is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short deliberately.
